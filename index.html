Projects

Toronto Coin Karaoke Platform

The Coin Karaoke is a newly trending Karaoke system in Asia. The traditional karaoke system restricts customers to pay full hours, usually minimum of one hour, regardless if they want to leave early. However, the Coin Karaoke will solve this problem as it allows customers to choose their own time to come and enjoy karaoke until they want. Instead of paying for restricted time for karaoke, customers will be using RFID card to charge, track and use their credits to operate our system. Customers will be paying per song by tapping their card to a machine. With this technology, customers could leave any time they want without time restriction, but enjoy karaoke for however many songs they want to sing.
